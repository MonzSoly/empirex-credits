AI reconstructions and dramatization

  • Wendelin Wiedeking (Porsche CEO, 1993–2009) and Martin Winterkorn (Volkswagen CEO, 2007–2015) appear as AI-generated character reconstructions on animated bodies. They are not photographs of these people and are labelled on screen as AI reconstructions.
  • The hedge-fund trader is a fictional, AI-generated character.
  • The scenes are dramatized. The announcement, debt statement, takeover papers and share certificate shown on screen are reconstructions, not archival documents. The 26 October 2008 headline quotes Porsche SE’s press release.
